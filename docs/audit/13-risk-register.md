# Risk register

## Rating method

- Likelihood: Low, Medium, or High based on the current static architecture and the amount of unresolved input.
- Impact: Moderate, High, or Critical for delivery, user safety, accessibility, identity, or recoverability.
- Evidence is classified with exactly one of the required audit classifications.
- Detection steps are for a later authorized implementation/verification phase. No build, server, submission, or external mutation was performed during this audit.
